# Two-sphere liquid bridge: experimental native DEM/PF-SPH stage

**Status:** The standalone two-sphere tests pass. The native `--case bridge`
SPHinXsys adapter has passed a C++ syntax/interface-stub check but has **not yet
been compiled or run against the pinned SPHinXsys checkout** in this environment.
The benchmark below is an initial numerical smoke test, **not a validated
capillary force, equilibrium meniscus, contact angle, or rupture criterion**.

## What this stage adds

- Two identical spherical DEM bodies at initial centers `x = ±(R+gap/2)`.
- A finite, gas-free PF-SPH liquid cylinder across the gap. The initial liquid
  lattice is cut out of the two sphere interiors. Its radius is `--radius`
  (**liquid cylinder transverse radius**, not outer droplet radius for this case);
  its initial half-length is `gap/2 + 0.65*R`.
- Initial two-sphere boundary samples are generated by the pinned SPHinXsys
  lattice generator as a **single** union solid body; solid sites are assigned
  to separate DEM bodies. This retains the existing one-to-one native contact
  relation and reciprocal liquid/solid force calculation.
- Option `--fixed-spheres 1` (default): fixed spheres for liquid-bridge
  relaxation and force measurement, while tracking the external support's
  linear/angular impulses. Option `0`: both spheres move under the *full* PF-SPH
  load and frictionless normal Hertz contact (`--contact-stiffness K`). The
  body centers, quaternion orientations and surface samples update every
  midpoint/end step.
- `pf_bridge.csv`: sphere separation/gap, count of near-midplane fluid samples,
  wetting counts on both spheres, a short-range liquid connectivity flag, minimum
  fluid-center clearance, full axial sphere loads, **direct solid–fluid PF
  forces**, remaining pressure/viscous components, Hertz contact energy.
- `pf_rigid.csv`: both bodies on separate rows per frame (appended `body_id`
  and `gap` columns, preserving original columns for one-body analysis).
- `PFSPH_CHECKPOINT 3`: adds bridge gap and contact stiffness to all existing
  version-2 state. Old v1/v2 **liquid and immersed** checkpoints remain readable.
  A *bridge* restart requires identical resolution, geometry, material, particle
  order, fixed/free flags and stiffness.
- A disconnected liquid neck is flagged using a liquid-liquid graph with pair
  distance `<1.6*dx`; counting particles alone would not establish connectivity.

## Build in your existing CLion project (Apple Silicon/vcpkg)

After extracting the complete ZIP, copy `sphinxsys-capillary/` contents to the
project root. Preserve the existing `deps/SPHinXsys` checkout and CLion's CMake
profiles; no dependency installation changes are required.

Select the `pf_capillary_validation`, `pf_bridge_test`, and `pf_restart_test`
targets in CLion, or run:

```bash
cmake --build cmake-build-release-vcpkgsph --target \
  pf_capillary_validation pf_bridge_test pf_restart_test -j 6
ctest --test-dir cmake-build-release-vcpkgsph \
  -R 'pf_(bridge_regression|restart_regression|bridge_adapter_smoke)' \
  --output-on-failure
```

Run the fixed-sphere bridge (short native test):

```bash
python3 tools/run_pf_bridge_smoke.py \
  --executable "$PWD/cmake-build-release-vcpkgsph/pf_capillary_validation" \
  --output results-pf-bridge-first --end-time 0.002 --threads 4
```

If successful, upload `results-pf-bridge-first.zip`. If not, upload the ZIP
containing `run.log` and `check.json`. The runner checks basic conservation,
liquid connectivity and absence of severe liquid/sphere interpenetration.

A second, **experimental moving-two-sphere** smoke (only after the fixed test
works):

```bash
python3 tools/run_pf_bridge_smoke.py \
  --executable "$PWD/cmake-build-release-vcpkgsph/pf_capillary_validation" \
  --fixed-spheres 0 --contact-stiffness 10 \
  --output results-pf-bridge-moving --end-time 0.002 --threads 4
```

The fixed run's `pf_bridge.csv` total loads are *support reaction loads on each
sphere*; a nonzero value during the short transient must **not** be presented
as equilibrium capillary adhesion. Direct PF loads are not the complete
capillary adhesion, because the pressure field also contributes.

## Key omissions and planned physical validation

1. `sff` and `sfs` remain raw pair-force strengths. Do NOT interpret them as
   physical surface tension `gamma`, Young contact angle `theta`, or calibrated
   solid adhesion. Calibrate `gamma` with a Laplace test and `theta` on an
   equilibrium flat solid before quantitative force comparisons.
2. No constant-liquid-volume continuation across different gaps. Changing
   `--bridge-gap` changes the initially occupied liquid volume for a fixed
   cylinder radius. Do not treat a raw gap sweep as a fixed-volume force curve.
3. No validated microscopic contact-line motion, impermeability, dynamic
   contact angle, bridge rupture, hysteresis, or multibody grain interactions
   with your soot DEM library. The initial union-sphere generator does not
   simulate persistent solid contacts or mesh-resolved wetting.
4. Verify time convergence, dx refinement, equilibrium meniscus shape, volume
   conservation, force symmetry and actual Young–Laplace reference. Initial
   diagnostics only test algebra and a short transient.
5. The 4dx-support neighbor graph is currently rebuilt each force evaluation;
   it remains the main performance bottleneck in the longer immersed test.
